Nikita Nikolaev

arXiv:2610.38972 [math.AG] · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.38972

This paper develops an intrinsic moduli theory of spectral structures on holomorphic vector bundles, relating them to pushforwards of line bundles along finite covers. It identifies their analytic moduli stack with a universal relative Picard stack and proves local existence of smooth spectral presentations in families. It also compares spectral structures with regular Higgs bundles and gives examples that do not arise from any Higgs field.

arXiv:2610.20123 [math-ph] · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.20123

This paper constructs exact WKB solutions for the Painlevé I system and uses them to solve its direct monodromy problem and formulate a normalised Riemann–Hilbert problem. The resulting Borel-summed solutions are identified with the five rotated tritronquée solutions.

arXiv:2603.18842 [math.AG] · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.18842

The authors develop a categorical framework for decorated Betti moduli spaces, including boundary markings that encode irregular singularities. The framework is designed to clarify the relationship between descriptions using local systems, groupoid representations, character varieties, and monodromy data.

arXiv:2503.12290 [math.DG] · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.12290

This work proves that the formal perturbative solution of the deformed Painlevé I equation is resurgent. It describes the geometry of the associated Borel transform through a surface related to the Fermat quintic, revealing the locations of its Borel singularities and Stokes directions.

A geometric proof of the resurgence of formal WKB solutions to Schrödinger equations on Riemann surfaces. Using meromorphic vector-field flows, holomorphic Lie groupoids, and spectral curves, the paper describes the geometry of the Borel plane, its singularities, and Stokes rays.

arXiv:1909.04011 [math.CA] · doi:10.48550/arXiv.1909.04011

The paper establishes an upper-triangular normal form for certain singularly perturbed rank-two logarithmic differential systems, using gauge transformations compatible with Gevrey asymptotics. It also constructs families of Levelt filtrations whose limits recover relevant eigenspace decompositions.

This paper proves existence and uniqueness for exact WKB solutions of a broad class of second-order linear differential equations in the complex domain, including generic and closed WKB trajectories. The results also establish Borel summability in a substantial class of examples and give an explicit description of the Borel transform.
